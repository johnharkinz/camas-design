DIRECTION A — RESTRAINED
Index
Camas as a catalogue. Every tool is a numbered entry in a well-kept index — hairlines, a strict grid and one red mark doing all the pointing.
Why it fits
The collection is the brand. Numbering makes “a home for small tools” literal and gives every future tool a slot.
Distinctive
Editorial index structure, not cards. Personality comes from order and a single vermilion tick, not from colour fields.
Works well
Tools and extensions — it is already dense, quiet and legible. Tables and status read instantly.
May struggle
Marketing can feel cool and literary. Risk of drifting toward “tasteful but anonymous” if the numbering idea is diluted.
Scaling
Excellent. The same row, rule and number pattern runs from homepage index to 360px popup without change.
