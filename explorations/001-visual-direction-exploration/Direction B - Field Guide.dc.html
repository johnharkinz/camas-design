Direction B — not-software
Field Guide
Camas is a wildflower. The tools are specimens, described with a naturalist’s patience: what it is, where you find it, what to look out for.
Why it fits
“Made by someone who cares how the web works” — observation, explanation, labelling. It treats the web as a living thing worth studying.
Distinctive
Serif voice, specimen plates, herbarium tags, sentence-style results. Nothing about it says SaaS.
Works well
Marketing and Shareable — diagnostics written as plain-English annotations make technical findings approachable.
May struggle
Popups: serif at 12px is fragile, so the tool UI leans on the sans. Needs real botanical illustration to land; without it, can feel twee.
Scaling
Good, with a deliberate shift: serif carries brand and headings; the grotesk carries controls. The tag shape and violet keep the family visible in the extension.
