Camas, as a system
The index lives at the family level — the name, the numbers, the site. Inside the tools it steps back: the family shows through type, rules, paper and ink, and the interface gets on with the task.
decisions in this pass
MetaphorFull dictionary entry on the homepage only. Numbers appear on product marks, lockups and the site index — never on rows, headers or checks.
Thumb indexRemoved from tools. Kept once, on camas.dev, as the site’s index of tools.
DepthTwo square, hard-edged cues. Raised (2px ink edge) means you can press it. Inset (soft inner shade) means it holds a value. Everything else is flat paper.
VermilionMeans “look here”: the index mark, problems and the focus ring. On/active states are ink, so “live” never looks like “wrong”.
FindingsWhat happened → why it matters → evidence. The evidence is always a readout, set inset and in mono.

Mono is for things the machine said or will send: header names and values, tag names, URLs, status codes, sizes. Also small meta labels. Never for headings, buttons or explanations.

Extensions live on 4–16. Tools on 8–32. The site uses 24–72 for section rhythm. Row heights: 36 (Bonce), 44 (Noggin), 48+ (web).
depth — function, not style
Raised — press it. A 2px hard ink edge below. Buttons, switch knobs, selected segment. Inset — holds a value. Inputs, switch tracks, evidence readouts. Flat — layout. Sections, lists, cards are rules on paper. No drop shadows anywhere.
rules
Ink rule — starts or ends a section. Hairline — between rows. Dashed — not yet: future entry, empty slot, draft. Left bar — the current / active item.
Corners are square everywhere. No radius tokens exist.
status — shape + word, colour third

buttons
Primary is ink, one per view. Pressed loses its edge and drops 2px. Disabled goes flat and dashed — it can’t be pressed, so it isn’t raised.

Square switches. On = solid ink track (the state you can read from across a room). Off = inset track with a raised knob waiting to be pushed. Always paired with a word in dense views.
