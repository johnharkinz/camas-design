# Explorations

This folder contains visual and product-design explorations for Camas.

Explorations are working material used to test possible directions before decisions are made.

They may include:

- visual identity concepts
- typography and colour experiments
- homepage concepts
- Shareable screens
- Noggin screens
- Bonce screens
- component treatments
- layout studies
- Claude Design outputs
- comparisons between competing directions

Explorations are **not part of the accepted design system by default**.

A design becomes canonical only when the relevant decisions have been reviewed and recorded in `docs/decisions/`.

When adding an exploration, try to include enough context to understand:

- what question the exploration is trying to answer
- what direction or idea it represents
- what works well
- what feels weak or unresolved
- whether it was rejected, developed further or accepted

Do not delete useful rejected directions simply because they were not chosen. They can provide valuable context for later decisions.
